Method in one paragraph

Posts are collected per exchange from two listed sources, scored with a transparent local lexicon/VADER method (English, Chinese and Korean market terms — see README), aggregated per ticker per trading day with engagement weighting, merged with Exa news sentiment (or the labelled RSS fallback) and price features, and fed to a pooled logistic-regression / ridge model per exchange. Support/resistance are classic pivot levels from the previous session plus 20-day swing extremes. Full details, code and limitations: see the project README.
